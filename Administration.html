Administrative Departments

Da'awah Committee

Responsible for Islamic propagation, lectures, outreach programmes and spiritual activities.

Media & Publicity

Handles photography, graphics, social media management and publicity of programmes.

Academic Committee

Organizes tutorials, academic seminars and educational development programmes.

Welfare Committee

Ensures the welfare and wellbeing of Muslim students across different faculties and departments.

Organizing Committee

The Organizing Committee is responsible for planning, cordinating, and ensuring the smooth execution of all MSSN programs and activities.THE Committee oversees venue arrangements,logistics,event scheduling,protocol and general programmme Management

Finance Committee

The Finance Committee manages the financial affairs of the society.It is responsible for budgeting, fund raising, financial planning,record keeping and ensuring that funds are utilized transparently and responsibly for the for the benefit of the Society

Masjid and timing Committee

The Masjid and timing committee oversees the maintenance and proper use of the MSSN Mosque facilities

Junndullah Committee

The Jundullah Committee is responsible for maintaining order, discipline and security during MSSN programs and Activities. The Committee asssists in crowd management, protocol enforcement, and ensuring a peaceful atmosphere at all events

Mu'azzins

The Mu'azzins committee is responsible for calling the adhan (call to prayer ).

Secretariat

The Secretariat serves as the Administrative and communication hub of the society. it is responsible for Documentation,correspondence,record keeping,meeting minutes, repport preparation , and maintaining official records of MSSN Activities and decisions
