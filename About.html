About MSSN IBBUL

Learn more about the mission, vision and activities of the Muslim Students Society of Nigeria, Ibrahim Badamasi Babangida University Chapter.

Who We Are

Muslim Students Society of Nigeria

MSSN IBBUL is a student Islamic organization established to promote Islamic values, brotherhood, academic excellence and spiritual development among Muslim students within Ibrahim Badamasi Babangida University.

The society organizes lectures, enlightenment programmes, Da'awah activities, humanitarian services and leadership development programmes aimed at building responsible Muslim students in the society.

Through unity and sincerity, MSSN IBBUL continues to contribute positively to campus life and the Muslim Ummah.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To nurture Muslim students spiritually, academically and morally through Islamic teachings, Da'awah and educational programmes.

Our Vision

To build a united and disciplined Muslim student community committed to Islam, knowledge and positive societal impact.

Our Objectives

Promoting unity, Islamic awareness, leadership development, academic excellence and peaceful coexistence among students.
