Shurah & Transition

A structured and organized transition process for selecting and preparing the next administration of MSSN IBBUL.

Shurah Information

Purpose

The Shurah process aims to identify qualified, responsible and dedicated Muslim students capable of leading the society effectively.

Screening Process

Screening will be conducted physically through interviews, consultations and assessment by the screening committee.

Eligibility

Candidates must possess good character, commitment to Islam, active participation in MSSN programmes and leadership qualities.

Transition Timeline

Week 1 - Announcement

Official announcement of Shurah exercise, available positions and screening guidelines.

Week 2 - Registration

Submission of candidate information and nomination details through the official platform.

Week 3 - Physical Screening

Candidates appear before the screening committee for physical interviews and consultations.

Week 4 - Walimah & Handover

Official handing over ceremony and Walimah programme for outgoing and incoming executives.
