About The Administration

This website serves as the official digital platform of the MSSN IBBUL 2025/2026 administration. It contains information about our programmes, Da'awah activities, weekly enlightenment, sisters forum, media activities, leadership transition, and all Islamic engagements within and outside the campus.

Our Major Programmes

Weekly Enlightenment

Weekly Islamic lectures and discussions held every Sunday for spiritual and academic development.

Sisters Forum

Special programmes organized every Saturday for sisters, focusing on Islamic morals and personal development.

Da'awah Week

A full week of Islamic activities including debates, humanitarian visitation, lectures, quiz competitions and more.
